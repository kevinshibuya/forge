# R10. LinkedIn, from primary sources

Research note for the forge v2 brief (research contract R10). Evidence gathered by a scout on
2026-09-24 from Microsoft Learn (API versions up to 202609) and LinkedIn legal pages; verdict and
reading by the main session (fable). [secondary] marks non-official sources.

## Verdict

Publishing text, image, document and multi-image posts to a member's profile is documented under the
Community Management (CM) scopes, and member post analytics exist. Two things the brief assumed do not
hold: the API cannot import a member's post history (the scope for it, `r_member_social`, is restricted
and not in the CM grant), so onboarding import is the member's own export upload; and daily per-post
analytics for a 20-member beta does not fit the Development tier's caps unless polling is narrowed to
recent posts plus the aggregate finder. Three things need answers from LinkedIn before code: whether a
Development-tier app gets programmatic refresh tokens, whether the 2022 API Terms' "must not automate
posting" clause binds CM apps, and whether a sole proprietor counts as a "registered legal organization".

## Evidence

1. CM grant: `w_member_social`, `w_member_social_feed`, `r_basicprofile`, `r_member_postAnalytics`
   (from 202506), `r_member_profileAnalytics` (from 202504). `r_member_social` is not in the list.
   [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/increasing-access, ms.date 2026-07-28]
2. Development tier: 500 calls per app per 24h, 100 per member per 24h, no BATCH_GET, webhooks
   disabled, 12 months to build. Standard: "No restrictions", separate application; LinkedIn "might not
   upgrade even if [you] meet these minimum requirements". [same]
3. Development-tier application: a registered legal organization, commercial use only; a verified
   business email ("Personal email addresses won't pass"); legal name, registered address, website,
   privacy policy; app verified by a super admin of the org's LinkedIn Page; no "Linked"/"In" or
   Microsoft marks in the name. A rejected app cannot re-apply; create a new one. Standard adds a full
   integration, test credentials and a narrated screencast per use case; the nearest use cases are
   "Executive Management" and "Employee Advocacy" (member posts to own profile via OAuth, aggregate
   engagement). [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management-app-review,
   updated 2026-02-11]
4. Timelines: "4-8 weeks at best, 3-4 months on average"; rejections "common and rarely explained";
   over-asking scopes is a named cause [secondary, vendor selling an alternative].
   [https://www.getphyllo.com/post/linkedin-api-access-in-2026-partner-program-approval-timeline-alternatives, 2026]
5. Posts API organic content types: text, image, video, document, article, multiImage, poll,
   celebration. Organic carousel not supported (sponsored only). Article posts get no URL scraping: title,
   description and thumbnail are set by the caller, thumbnail via the Images API. Writing needs
   `w_member_social`; no documented per-member daily post limit beyond 429.
   [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/shares/posts-api, ms.date 2026-05-07]
6. `GET /rest/posts?author=urn:li:person:{id}&q=author` exists, but "to retrieve all posts authored by
   a person, `r_member_social` permission is required", which is "restricted and available to approved
   users only". [same]
7. Documents API: initializeUpload with a person owner, PUT to `uploadUrl`, post with `content.media.id`;
   PDF, PPT(X), DOC(X) up to 100 MB and 300 pages; status WAITING_UPLOAD → PROCESSING → AVAILABLE.
   [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/shares/documents-api, ms.date 2026-06-19]
8. `memberCreatorPostAnalytics`: one queryType per call; `entity` finder for one post; `me` finder
   aggregates across the member's posts. Metrics from 202604: IMPRESSION, MEMBERS_REACHED, RESHARE,
   REACTION, COMMENT, POST_SAVE, POST_SEND, LINK_CLICKS, PREMIUM_CTA_CLICKS, FOLLOWER_GAINED_FROM_CONTENT,
   PROFILE_VIEW_FROM_CONTENT. DAILY unsupported for several metrics and for single-post impressions; `me`
   REACTION, COMMENT, RESHARE "not consistent with UI"; counts "best-effort".
   [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/members/post-statistics, ms.date 2026-05-11]
9. Tokens: access 60 days; programmatic refresh tokens 365 days, not extended on use, then
   re-authorisation; LinkedIn may revoke either at any time. Eligibility: "all approved Marketing
   Developer Platform (MDP) partners". [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/shared/authentication/programmatic-refresh-tokens, ms.date 2025-05-31]
10. Versioning: monthly versions, each supported at least one year; `Linkedin-Version: YYYYMM` required;
    202510 sunsets 2026-10-15; latest 202609. [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/versioning, ms.date 2026-09-16]
11. Data export: Shares.csv holds date, URL, the post text ("shared comments") and visibility; Articles
    URLs only; shares arrive in the later batch (24-48h); files expire after 72h.
    [https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1339364]
12. API Terms of Use (rev. 2022-12-13): "must not… use the Content or the APIs to automate posting on
    the LinkedIn Services"; "must not capture, copy, cache, or store any Content"; deletion duties do not
    apply to data "separately entered or uploaded to you by the User". No AI clause.
    [https://www.linkedin.com/legal/l/api-terms-of-use]
13. Marketing API Terms (rev. 2025-07-25), named by the CM review page as governing: no storage of
    Marketing Data beyond the Data Storage Requirements (§4.1); deletion within 10 days on request or
    client departure (§4.2); no automation or AI clause found. [https://www.linkedin.com/legal/l/marketing-api-terms]
14. Reach: scheduler vendors report no penalty for third-party publishing, mostly measured on Instagram;
    a claimed LinkedIn statement on parity could not be traced to an official source [secondary].

## Beta arithmetic (20 members, 5 posts a week each)

- Publishing: about 14 posts a day; text 1 call, image 2 calls plus a PUT to `dms-uploads` (whether it
  counts is not established); 15-45 calls a day against 500.
- Per-post analytics over a trailing 30 days: about 21 posts × 5 metrics ≈ 107 calls per member per
  day, over the 100 per-member cap, and 2,140 per app against 500. Fits if only posts younger than 7 days
  are polled (5 × 4 = 20 per member, 400 per app) with publishing on top at 440-445 of 500. Or: the `me`
  aggregate for 5 metrics (100 per app) plus a weekly per-post sweep. BATCH_GET is unavailable.

## Reading

The brief's decision 22 said "pull history through the API where a platform permits"; LinkedIn does not
permit it under CM, so onboarding on LinkedIn is the export upload, and claim 1 in `claims.md` stays
"pending" until the upload flow exists. The export's 24-72-hour delay means onboarding is a two-visit
flow on LinkedIn, and the Voice profile run waits for the file; X (R11) has no such delay. The
Development tier is workable for a beta only with a sparse pull schedule, and that schedule is the
same one R11's X pricing wants, so one design serves both: poll each post on a decaying schedule (days
1, 2, 3, 7, 14, 30), plus the LinkedIn `me` aggregate daily.

Three items are questions for LinkedIn, not for research, and each can stop the plan: item 12's
automation clause read literally forbids the CM API's own use case, item 9's refresh-token eligibility
decides whether scheduled publishing survives a 60-day token, and item 3's "registered legal
organization" decides whether Kevin can apply as a sole proprietor or needs a company first. The last
is the one to answer this week, because it gates the application and the application gates the beta.

## Decision fed

- Publish path (decision 15): Posts, Images and Documents APIs under `w_member_social`; carousels as PDF
  document posts; article links with caller-supplied title and thumbnail; `Linkedin-Version` bumped on
  a schedule.
- Onboarding import (decision 22): LinkedIn history comes from Shares.csv; the API path is X only.
- Outcomes (decision 17): decaying per-post schedule plus `me` aggregate; storage per the Data Storage
  Requirements (unread, open item).
- ADR 0002 amendment: the API is usable for analytics under CM approval; the export stays the import
  path and the fallback.
- Publish reliability (R16): without `r_member_social`, a crashed publish cannot be reconciled by
  listing the member's posts; the `needs_review` path is mandatory on LinkedIn.

## Open items (three are blocking)

- **Blocking**: refresh-token eligibility at Development tier; the 2022 automation clause versus CM
  apps (ask Developer Support); whether a sole proprietor qualifies as a registered legal organization.
- Restricted-use-cases page and Data Storage Requirements page not read.
- Whether `dms-uploads` PUTs count against the cap; Images API and multiImage size limits.
- Whether the User Agreement permits handing one's export to a third party (not found in §8.2).

## Confidence

High on scopes, caps, token TTLs, versioning and the missing history import (primary docs). Medium on
the beta arithmetic. Low on approval timelines and reach effects.
