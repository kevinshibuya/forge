# R6. Jev on forge's tasks

Research note for the forge v2 brief (research contract R6). Evidence gathered by a scout on
2026-09-24; verdict and reading by the main session (fable). Kevin asked for this question directly.

## Verdict

No external measurement covers any of forge's three candidate tasks (Tier triage, platform-fit rubric
scoring, Tell pre-filtering), Jev's training is English-primary with Portuguese unsupported, and the
independent evaluations that exist show it reaching parity with Haiku 4.5 only after decomposition into
several signal questions plus a small trained head on about a thousand labels. Jev is out of v1. A
gated trial on Triage stays possible later and is cheap; platform fit and Tells have no evidence behind
them, and Tells already have a deterministic lexicon that is the simpler baseline.

## Evidence

1. jev-1.13.0 limits: 64k request tokens, 32k for state plus the longest question; Choice up to 255
   options; Score 2-10 levels; no string output, no images. Documented weaknesses: arithmetic,
   counting, dates, multi-step reasoning, mixed judgements, adversarial text; **option order affects
   results**; pin the version when tuning confidence bars.
   [https://pydantic.dev/docs/ai/models/typesafe/, read 2026-09-24]
2. $0.042 per 1M input tokens, output free; TypeSafe says the price may be subsidised. Its own
   workflow benchmark: Jev 76.0% vs GPT-6 Sol 79.1% vs Opus 5 78.4%, at 0.4 s vs 34.3 s vs 92.1 s, on
   workflows TypeSafe designed [vendor].
   [https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/typesafe-jev-system-one-models-release-guide-2026, 2026-09-16]
3. Rate limits "currently fluctuate due to high demand"; training English-primary.
   [https://github.com/bug-ops/zeph/issues/6801, 2026-09-22, quoting vendor docs]
4. Independent phishing eval, 2,000 emails: one question asked once, Jev 62.6% vs Haiku 4.5 81.3%,
   56.8% false negatives (McNemar p<0.0001). Decomposed into 5 signal questions plus logistic
   regression on 1,000 labels: 95.0% vs Haiku 93.2% (p=0.063, not significant). Wrong criteria
   descriptions dropped accuracy to 16.7%, below the 25% random baseline. Out-of-distribution ECE 0.107
   vs 0.024-0.032 on public benchmarks. On an unknowable task, 44.7% accuracy at 0.74 mean confidence.
   Yes/no underconfident; Choice and Score overconfident. State text not treated as hostile (prompt
   injection risk). Single US West Coast region.
   [https://www.beri.net/article/typesafe-jev-typed-decision-model-calibration-decomposition-shadow-eval, 2026-09-20]
5. Agent tool-call risk, 60 cases: 91.7% asked once; 95.1%, AUROC 0.988 with 5 signals plus a head.
   [https://dev.to/webofmike/i-benchmarked-jev-on-agent-tool-call-risk-calibration-held-49i3]
6. Indexed but unverified: CLINC150 84.75% auto-routed at 2.25% in-scope loss; KoBBQ "unknown" on 95%
   of ambiguous items; Banking77 / Web of Science 500 items each; JevBench publishes scoring code. An
   official TypeScript SDK exists under github.com/typesafe-ai.
   [https://github.com/AbdelStark/awesome-typesafe-jev]
7. Cloudflare Workers AI hosts `typesafe/jev` with a byte-identical schema; Vercel and LangChain
   integrate it. [Forbes, 2026-09-19]
8. Literature (cited from memory, unverified): fine-tuned small classifiers beat zero-shot LLMs on text
   classification when labels exist (Bucher & Martini 2024; Edwards & Camacho-Collados 2024). Jev is
   zero-shot with no fine-tuning, and item 4 shows it needs decomposition plus a trained head for parity.

## Reading

The sibling project's measurements (5/5 on plain labelling, 0/29 on citation checking, a net loss as a
reranker) and the independent phishing eval say the same thing: Jev answers closed, well-criterioned,
surface-level questions well, and anything requiring the model to compare texts or hold an implicit
standard poorly. Tier triage is the only forge task of the first kind, and forge's triage already runs
on Sonnet at about $0.03 per idea (R12). The saving is real in latency (0.4 s versus tens of seconds)
and negligible in money at beta volume. Portuguese being unsupported removes half of v1's Voices from
the trial anyway.

## Decision fed

- Jev does not enter v1.
- Optional later trial, Triage only: one yes/no per Tier, criteria written and frozen, option order
  shuffled, version pinned; labelled blind sample of at least 150 ideas stratified to 50 per Tier (a
  Wilson interval of about ±5 pp at p=0.90 needs n≈139); auto-route only above a confidence T fixed on
  a 60% calibration split with accuracy ≥ 0.90; report coverage and accuracy above T on the held-out
  40%; McNemar against the incumbent Sonnet triage. Trigger for the trial: triage latency or cost
  becomes a felt problem.
- Platform-fit scoring: deterministic contract check plus a small LLM rubric judge (R5); not Jev.
- Tell pre-filter: the lexicon and regex lint is the baseline; not Jev.

## Open items

- Portuguese support; official limitations page and rate limits; TS SDK package name; deprecation
  policy (undocumented per item 4).

## Confidence

Medium on product facts (several sources agree). Low on transfer to forge's tasks (no direct evidence).
